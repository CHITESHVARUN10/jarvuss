These are authored synthetic examples, not collected private transcripts. The
generators exclude normalized exact matches with older shards and all held-out
benchmark files. Near-template reuse is measured separately. Fractional volume
labels exercise raw model interpretation; the current native volume parser does
not resolve every fractional phrase. Offline action-mapping reports expose that
limitation rather than counting unmappable gold labels as executable successes.

`training/generate_status_shard.py`.
